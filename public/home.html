Riff Catalog

Record your guitar riffs, keep them organized, and practice with them. Free, and no music-theory degree required.

Open Riff Catalog

Works in your web browser on a phone, tablet or computer. On an iPhone or iPad, use Share → Add to Home Screen to install it like an app.

What it is for

Every guitar player has a phone full of half-remembered ideas. Riff Catalog gives them a home. Hit record when an idea shows up, give it a name, and find it again later instead of scrolling through a camera roll.

What you can do

  • Record a riff in one tap, even without an internet connection. It uploads when you’re back online.
  • Keep a library of your recordings with names, tags, keys and tempos, and filter or sort to find the one you want.
  • Find a key and see which notes and chords fit, on a fretboard or piano.
  • Practice with random picks so you stop playing the same three things.
  • Jam along to drum beats you can build and save.
  • Spark an idea with the randomizer when you’re stuck.

Your music stays yours

You sign in with your Google account. Riff Catalog saves your recordings and notes in your own Google Drive, and it can only see the files and folders it created itself. It can’t look at anything else in your Drive.

We never see or keep your recordings. We only keep simple usage numbers, such as how often each part of the app is used, so we know what to improve. Read the full privacy policy.

Questions or trouble?

Open the app, choose Support from the menu, and send us a message. We reply by email.
